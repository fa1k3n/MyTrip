# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately, not in a public issue. Send an email to **security@falkenproduktion.se** with:

- what you found and where (the page, the address or the file),
- the steps to reproduce it, and what you expected to happen,
- what an attacker could gain from it, if you know.

Write in Swedish or English. You will get an answer within 5 working days, with a first assessment and what happens next. Please give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you publish anything about it.

The same contact is listed in [/.well-known/security.txt](https://baslager.app/.well-known/security.txt) on the app.

## Scope

In scope: the Basläger app at baslager.app and the code in this repository.

Out of scope: denial-of-service tests, spam, social engineering of the people who run the service, and problems in third-party services (map, weather, currency, AI and mail providers), which should be reported to those providers.

## Good-faith research

We will not take legal action against anyone who reports a problem in good faith, follows this policy and does not access, change or keep other people's data beyond what is needed to show the problem.
